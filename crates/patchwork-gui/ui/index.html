                              My computers

                              Link the computers you play on so they all have the same profiles.

                              • Every profile on a linked computer shows up on the others, including ones you make later.
                              • When you change a profile on one computer, the others get the change the next time Patchwork (or background sync) is running on both.
                              • Profiles you've shared with friends, or joined from them, are included.

                              To link two computers, press Create ticket on one and paste the ticket on the other.

                              This computer is linked to your others.

                              Your other computers name them to tell them apart; names stay on this computer
                                Keep Patchwork open here until it has linked. The ticket lets whoever has it change every profile here, so don't give it to anyone else.

                                Background

                                Keeps working after you close Patchwork: synced profiles still send and receive changes, and mod updates are still checked for. It starts when you log in, and each job below can be turned off.
