                        My computers

                        Linked computers keep every profile in step: profiles on any of them, including ones made later, are on all of them, and a change on one goes to the rest. Profiles you share with friends or follow come along too.

                        This computer is linked to your others.

                        Keep Patchwork open here until it has linked. The ticket lets whoever has it change every profile here, so don't give it to anyone else.

                        Background sync

                        Keeps synced profiles in step even while Patchwork is closed. It starts when you log in, and steps aside while Patchwork or the patchwork command is open, since they sync by themselves. Changes that come in are downloaded, but never deployed.
